ℹ️ About AquaSense

AquaSense is a proposed low-cost portable system for preliminary water-quality screening and wastewater treatment monitoring.

Actual measurements require suitable sensors, calibration and validation. These readings alone cannot establish treatment effectiveness or drinking-water safety.
